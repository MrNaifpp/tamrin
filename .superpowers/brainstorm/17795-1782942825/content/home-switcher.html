Home screen: how do you switch workspaces?

The app is always "inside" one workspace (Slack-style). The card feed you already built stays — the question is where the switcher lives. Mockups are RTL/Arabic like the real app. Click the one that feels right.

A — Workspace name as the title

The nav-bar title is the current workspace. Tapping it drops a sheet with your workspaces + "create". Zero new chrome — reuses your glass-pill toolbar. Switching is 2 taps.

B — Avatar button + switcher sheet

A workspace avatar (colored initial) sits in the corner; tapping opens a half-sheet switcher with avatars, like Slack/Discord. More visual identity per group, one extra element in the bar.
